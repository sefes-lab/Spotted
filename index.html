How to Play
Spotted! — License Plate Spotter Game
🚗 What is Spotted?
Look out the car window for license plates from different US states and D.C. When you see one — yell SPOTTED! and tap it in the app. The further away the state is, the more points you earn!
▶️ Getting Started
1Tap New Spot Run on the home screen
2Give your trip a name (e.g. "Summer Vacation")
3Choose your Home Turf — the state you live in
4Add the names of everyone playing
5Tap Let's Spot! 🔦 and start looking!
🎯 Spotting a Plate
When you see a license plate from another state:
1Find that state on the Spot Grid or Map
2Tap it!
3Choose who spotted it if playing with others
4Watch your score go up 🎉
💡 You can spot the same state more than once — every sighting earns points!
⭐ How Points Work
Your Home Turf state scores 0 points — you see those plates all the time! Nearby states score fewer points. Far away or rare states score the most.
1 pt — Neighboring states
4–5 pts — Mid-distance states
7–8 pts — Far away states
10 pts — Alaska, Hawaii, D.C. 🔥
🌡️ On the map, cool blue = close, hot red = far!

👥 Playing Together
Add multiple Spotters to a run — one for each person in the car. When someone spots a plate, select their name. The Spotter Leaderboard in HQ shows who's winning!
🏅 Each spotter earns their own score. May the best spotter win!

Earn special badges as you play! Check the HQ tab to see all 17 achievements. Can you unlock them all?
